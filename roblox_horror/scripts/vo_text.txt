If a Roblox server says you're alone... don't believe the player list.

It was three in the morning. I joined an old obby that nobody had touched in years. The sun was bright. The music was cheerful. And the server was empty.

Then the chat said: "Welcome back."

A player stood on the spawn pad. Smiling. Not moving. His name was Player Zero.

Rule one. Never look away from him.

I blinked. He was ten studs closer.

Rule two. Never answer in chat. I typed one word. "Who?" The music slowed down.

I ran. Over the lava, over the neon bridges. Every time I turned around... he was there. Smiling.

The sky turned red. The platforms began to vanish beneath my feet.

Rule three. Never leave the server.

I pressed leave. The button wasn't there. I looked at the player list. His name was gone. The only name left... was mine. And it said: Player Zero.

The chat said: "Welcome back."

And far away, on the spawn pad... someone new had joined. Smiling.
